Pixel Arcana: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 (version 1.1)

Pixel Arcana is a tarot card game made by Game Dev Hub, based in Bangkok, Thailand. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: we collect no personal information ourselves. From version 1.1 the app can show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to do that, as described below.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (Stardust, readings, shop items, and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. The iOS system backup may include this storage if you have backups enabled.

Information we collect

Game Dev Hub collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. We run no server that receives your data.

Ads (version 1.1 and later)

When you run out of candles you can choose to watch a short rewarded video ad to light one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Game Dev Hub does not receive this data.

On first launch the app may ask for your consent (in regions that require it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) and then asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, ads can still appear but will not be personalised, and everything else works the same. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and, where it applies, in the app under Settings → Ad choices.

You can avoid ads entirely: they are optional, and the one-time purchase "Eternal Flame" removes the need for them by giving unlimited candles. Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Optional purchases (candles, word and spread packs, looks and fortune tellers) are processed by Apple through the App Store. Game Dev Hub does not receive your payment details or your Apple ID. The app can ask Apple whether you already own a purchase, which is how Restore Purchases works.

Network use

The game itself runs offline and does not send your game data to any server. The app goes online only for purchases (to Apple) and to load and show ads (to Google).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Ads shown in the app are not personalised for children, and the app asks for tracking permission only through Apple's standard prompt.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's store listing.
