Pixel Arcana: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Pixel Arcana is a tarot card game made by Game Dev Hub, based in Bangkok, Thailand. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app does not collect personal information, does not track you, and does not show ads.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (Stardust, readings, shop items, and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. The iOS system backup may include this storage if you have backups enabled.

Information we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Game Dev Hub does not receive your payment details or your Apple ID. The app can ask Apple whether you already own a purchase, which is how Restore Purchases works.

Network use

The game runs offline. The app does not send your data to any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's store listing.
